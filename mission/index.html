MISSION STATEMENT

BRIGHAM YOUNG UNIVERSITY-IDAHO


Brigham Young University-Idaho was founded and is supported and guided bu The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Its mission is to develope decisiples of Jesus Christ who are leaders in their homes, the Church, and their communities.

The university does this by:

  1. 1.Buliding testimonies of the restored gospel of Jesus Christ and fostering its principles in a wholesome academic, cultural, and social environment

  2. 2.Providing a high-quality education that prepares students of diverse interests and abilities for lifelong learning and employment

  3. 3.Serving as many students as possible within resource constraints

  4. 4.Delivering education that is affordable for students and the Church
